Who we are

Your point of contact.
Across the supply process.

Agro Alliance Trading Pte Ltd is a Singapore-based fertilizer trading company serving importers, distributors and fertilizer blenders.

We bring together product sourcing, commercial coordination and shipment planning. Whether you are enquiring about a single product or several materials for your market, our Singapore trade desk is your point of contact for the discussion.

Our range includes urea, ammonium sulphate, ammonium chloride, potassium nitrate, calcium nitrate and custom NPK. Available origins, grades, packaging and shipment windows are reviewed against each enquiry.

Our manufacturing background in Fujian supports our understanding of fertilizer production and handling. Our sourcing scope also extends to other origins, with the supply route agreed around your product and destination.

Our background

A trading company.
A manufacturing foundation.

Agro Alliance was established in Singapore in 2024 for international fertilizer trade. Our manufacturing roots trace back to Xingda Fertilizer Factory, founded in Putian, Fujian in 1988, and the expansion to Fuqing Jiangyin as Fujian Yaoda Fertilizer in 2012.

Today, Fujian Yaoda Fertilizer Technology Co., Ltd. forms part of our manufacturing background. The Fuqing base supports our wider supply network, while Agro Alliance coordinates buyer enquiries, commercial requirements and shipment planning.

Supply & shipping

From your requirements
to an agreed shipment plan.

Every enquiry starts with the product and the market it needs to serve.

  1. 01

    Define the requirement

    Share the product, required grade, quantity in metric tons, packaging and destination. For custom NPK, include the nutrient ratio and any requirements for granule size or colour.

    Product · Specification · Quantity
  2. 02

    Review supply options

    We discuss available origins, product forms and shipment windows against your requirements. Urea is a current supply focus alongside our established fertilizer range; allocation and availability are confirmed at the time of enquiry.

    Origin · Packaging · Availability
  3. 03

    Agree the commercial details

    Align the quotation, specification, payment terms, inspection requirements and supporting documents. Requirements vary by product and destination, so the agreed scope is established for the individual order.

    Quotation · Documents · Inspection
  4. 04

    Coordinate the shipment

    Provide your destination port and delivery window. We coordinate the origin, loading port and export documentation around the agreed shipment plan. Routes, sailing availability and lead times are confirmed per order.

    Loading port · Destination · Timing

Specifications & documents

The details that matter
for your order.

Documentation and inspection needs differ by product, origin and market.

Product specification

Confirm the grade, nutrient content, product form and packaging before ordering. Website grades and sample photographs are reference information; the agreed specification defines the order.

Supporting documents

Ask for the current product specification, Certificate of Analysis and relevant manufacturing certificates. Let us know which documents your market or customer requires so they can be reviewed for the proposed supply.

Samples & inspection

Discuss product samples and third-party inspection requirements with our trade desk. Availability, sample delivery and inspection arrangements are agreed for each enquiry.

Payment & shipment terms

Payment terms, including letter-of-credit arrangements where applicable, are confirmed in the quotation and contract alongside the shipment and documentation requirements.
