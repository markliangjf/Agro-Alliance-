Your enquiry and privacy.

This page explains how information is used when you contact our trade desk or browse this website.

Information you choose to send

The enquiry form asks for your name, company, email, product and destination. Quantity and additional shipment requirements help us respond but can be confirmed later. Please do not include payment card details, identity documents or other sensitive information in the form.

How an enquiry reaches us

When website submission is enabled, the form uses Formspree to pass your enquiry to our trade desk at info@agro-alliance.sg. Formspree processes submitted information as part of providing that service. See Formspree's privacy policy.

Alternatively, you can prepare an email draft or WhatsApp message. These options open the corresponding application; you must press Send there to deliver the message. Their providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Why we use the information

We use your details to respond to your enquiry, clarify specifications and discuss a potential order. Information needed to review supply may be shared with the relevant supplier or service provider for that purpose. Sending an enquiry does not subscribe you to a marketing mailing list.

Optional website analytics

Optional Google Analytics is not currently enabled on this website.

When enabled with your permission, analytics distinguish product views, contact clicks, attempted submissions and submissions accepted by the enquiry service. We do not include names, email addresses, companies, destinations, quantities or free-text messages in these events. A contact click is not recorded as a received enquiry, and acceptance by the form service is not proof of email delivery or a completed sale.

Your analytics choice is remembered in this browser for up to 180 days. Declining analytics does not affect the enquiry form. We honour supported Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals.

Your choices and questions

Contact info@agro-alliance.sg if you want to ask about, correct or request deletion of information you have provided. We review requests in light of any information that needs to be retained for business or record-keeping purposes.
