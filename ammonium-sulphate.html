Common questions

Before you request a quote.

Do you handle both crystal and granular enquiries?+

Both forms are included in our product range. Specify the form you need; availability and the proposed specification are confirmed when your enquiry is reviewed.

Can I request samples or inspection?+

Yes. Tell us the product form and your requirements. Sample availability, delivery and third-party inspection arrangements are agreed for the proposed supply.

What if I need documents for a particular market?+

Include your list when enquiring. Supporting documentation is reviewed for the individual product, origin and destination; the website does not promise one universal set of certificates.
