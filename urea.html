Before we quote

Three details to start with.

A first enquiry does not need a complete specification sheet.

01 / PRODUCT

Your required specification

Granular urea is listed in our range at an indicative nitrogen content of 46%. Send your requested grade and any limits for moisture, biuret or particle size so the proposed specification can be checked against your needs.

02 / VOLUME

Quantity and packing

Give an estimated quantity in metric tons, or tell us it is still being confirmed. Include your preferred bag size or bulk handling requirement. Packaging, minimum quantities and loading arrangements depend on the proposed supply.

03 / DESTINATION

Where and when

Include the destination port or country and your preferred shipment window. Tell us whether you want to discuss FOB, CFR or CIF terms. Origin, availability and shipment details are confirmed in the quotation.
