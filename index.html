Our Story

Backed by a 500,000-Ton
Manufacturing Powerhouse

Agro Alliance Trading Pte Ltd is the Singapore-based international trading arm of Fujian Yaoda Fertilizer Technology Co., Ltd. — a major Chinese granular fertilizer manufacturer specialising in ammonium sulphate, ammonium chloride and NPK fertilizers.

Operating from a 60,000+ m² facility in Fuqing Jiangyin Industrial Zone, the manufacturing base runs three production lines with a combined 500,000-ton annual capacity. Approximately 200,000 tons are exported annually — equivalent to over 10,000 standard containers shipped through Jiangyin Port each year.

Our Singapore presence enables seamless international trade finance, dedicated logistics coordination, and direct market access for buyers worldwide.

1988
Founded as Xingda Fertilizer Factory (兴达化肥厂) in Putian, Fujian — one of the region's earliest compound fertilizer producers
2012
Relocated and expanded to Fuqing Jiangyin Industrial Zone — 60,000+ m² facility with three production lines, rebranded as Fujian Yaoda Fertilizer Technology Co., Ltd.
2017
Pivoted to international exports under China's fertilizer export policy — began shipping to Southeast Asia via Jiangyin Deep-Water Port
2024
Established Agro Alliance Trading Pte Ltd in Singapore as the dedicated international trading arm, enabling direct global market access
