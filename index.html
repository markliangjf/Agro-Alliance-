Before you enquire
A few useful details.


What should I include in a quotation request?+
Include the product and grade, estimated quantity in metric tons, packaging preference, destination port and delivery timing. Add any inspection or documentation requirements so they can be included in the quotation.


Can I request custom NPK fertilizer?+
Yes. Share your required N:P:K ratio, granule size, colour and packaging. Availability, minimum order quantity and production lead time are confirmed after reviewing the specification.


Can I request samples and quality documents?+
Ask our trade desk about product samples, the current specification and Certificate of Analysis. Sample delivery and third-party inspection arrangements are agreed according to your requirements.


Which shipment and payment terms are available?+
FOB, CFR and CIF terms can be discussed for your destination. Payment terms, including letter-of-credit arrangements where applicable, are confirmed in the quotation and contract.
